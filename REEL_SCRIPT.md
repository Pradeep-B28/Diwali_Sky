# Diwali Sky: Video Reel Scripts

This document provides two recording scripts matched to demo mode. One is vertical (30 seconds) for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. One is horizontal (60 seconds) for YouTube and LinkedIn.

Neither script uses emojis. Neither script uses em dashes.

---

## Version 1: Vertical Reel (30 Seconds)

- **Format:** 9:16 vertical (1080 x 1920)
- **URL to record:** `http://localhost:8000/?demo=1&name=Priya`
- **Audio:** Original Web Audio sound generated by the app.

### Timeline and Shot List

#### 0.0 to 2.0 seconds: The Hook
- **Visual:** The deep navy night sky with flickering diyas and twinkling stars.
- **On-screen text:** What happens when you write a name in fireworks?
- **Voiceover (optional):** What happens when you write someone's name in fireworks?

#### 2.0 to 5.0 seconds: The Input
- **Visual:** The white card shows. A glowing tap dot lands on "Their name", types "Priya", and taps "Light it up".
- **On-screen text:** Type any name. Tap once.
- **Voiceover (optional):** Type their name and tap Light it up.

#### 5.0 to 8.0 seconds: The Ascent
- **Visual:** Three opening rockets launch with whistles and burst into peony, ring, and willow formations.
- **On-screen text:** Opening bursts over the night sky.
- **Voiceover (optional):** Rockets climb into the dark sky.

#### 8.0 to 12.0 seconds: The Formation
- **Visual:** The emerald hero rocket climbs to the center. It explodes with a deep boom. Thousands of sparks fly outward, curve, and assemble into the name "Priya".
- **On-screen text:** Thousands of sparks swirl into place.
- **Voiceover (optional):** One big shell bursts and its sparks fly into place to spell their name.

#### 12.0 to 16.0 seconds: The Hold Moment
- **Visual:** The name shimmers in the brand gradient. "Happy Diwali" glows in gold above. "from Vivek" appears below.
- **On-screen text:** Happy Diwali in the sky.
- **Voiceover (optional):** A personalized greeting held in mid-air.

#### 16.0 to 20.0 seconds: The Melt
- **Visual:** Sparks fall like a golden willow, leaving soft luminous trails.
- **On-screen text:** Pure code. No images. No video files.
- **Voiceover (optional):** Then it melts into falling gold sparks.

#### 20.0 to 25.0 seconds: The Share Sheet
- **Visual:** The share card slides up showing "Send on WhatsApp" and the private link.
- **On-screen text:** Share privately on WhatsApp.
- **Voiceover (optional):** Send it to someone special in one tap.

#### 25.0 to 30.0 seconds: Closing
- **Visual:** Wrench Wise logo chip in focus with the starry sky behind.
- **On-screen text:** Built with code by Wrench Wise. Learn to build apps like this.
- **Voiceover (optional):** Built with code by Wrench Wise. Learn to build apps like this at wrench-wise.com.

## Version 2: Horizontal Video (60 Seconds)

- **Format:** 16:9 horizontal (1920 x 1080)
- **URL to record:** `http://localhost:8000/?demo=1&name=Priya`
- **Audio:** Original Web Audio sound generated by the app.

### Timeline and Shot List

#### 0.0 to 4.0 seconds: The Hook
- **Visual:** Opening wide shot of the dark sky, horizon glow, and flickering oil lamps.
- **On-screen text:** We built a firework name writer with zero libraries.
- **Voiceover (optional):** We built a web app that writes any name in fireworks using plain code.

#### 4.0 to 10.0 seconds: The Setup
- **Visual:** The white card shows. The demo dot types "Priya" into the input and taps "Light it up".
- **On-screen text:** No frameworks. No external audio files. Front-end only.
- **Voiceover (optional):** There are no frameworks, no libraries, and no audio files. Everything runs directly in the browser.

#### 10.0 to 16.0 seconds: Physics and Rockets
- **Visual:** Three opening rockets launch with rising whistles. Peony and willow bursts illuminate the sky.
- **On-screen text:** Real-time physics on a pre-allocated particle pool.
- **Voiceover (optional):** Pre-allocated typed arrays keep the animation running at 60 frames per second on mobile phones.

#### 16.0 to 24.0 seconds: The Shell Burst
- **Visual:** The emerald hero rocket ascends to the upper center. The shell detonates with a deep boom.
- **On-screen text:** The hero shell explodes into thousands of sparks.
- **Voiceover (optional):** The hero rocket reaches its peak and explodes into thousands of floating sparks.

#### 24.0 to 34.0 seconds: The Name Assembly
- **Visual:** Sparks curve through the air, homing into letter positions from left to right with a shimmering arrival sound.
- **On-screen text:** Every spark curves to its exact coordinate.
- **Voiceover (optional):** Each spark calculates its trajectory to land exactly where it belongs, spelling their name in the brand gradient.

#### 34.0 to 42.0 seconds: The Hold Moment
- **Visual:** The name shimmers at rest. "Happy Diwali" glows above in gold.
- **On-screen text:** Pure Web Audio synthesis. Procedural crackles and booms.
- **Voiceover (optional):** The sound effects are generated procedural tones created entirely using the Web Audio API.

#### 42.0 to 48.0 seconds: The Willow Melt
- **Visual:** Gravity pulls each spark downwards, transitioning from emerald to festive gold trails.
- **On-screen text:** Gravity pulls the sparks into golden trails.
- **Voiceover (optional):** When the hold ends, the letters melt into a shower of golden willow trails.

#### 48.0 to 54.0 seconds: Private Links
- **Visual:** The share panel slides up with WhatsApp and native share buttons.
- **On-screen text:** Names travel inside the URL hash. Never sent to any server.
- **Voiceover (optional):** The greeting travels inside the URL hash so names remain private and never touch a server.

#### 54.0 to 60.0 seconds: Closing
- **Visual:** End card with "Learn to build apps like this" link and Wrench Wise branding.
- **On-screen text:** Built with code by Wrench Wise. Visit wrench-wise.com.
- **Voiceover (optional):** Built with code by Wrench Wise. Learn how to build apps like this at wrench-wise.com.
